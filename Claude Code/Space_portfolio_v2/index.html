Research Paper RAG

RAG over 23 research PDFs with ChromaDB, FlashRank re-ranking and Langfuse tracing. On a held-out 100-question benchmark, re-ranking raised Hit@5 from 0.61 to 0.74.

Live demo

Tainted Evidence

Prompt injection in a Llama 3.3 70B claim verifier on SciFact. A planted reviewer note raised wrong SUPPORTS verdicts on contradicted claims from 6/80 to 16/80.

Atlas

A 4-agent LangGraph research report generator (FastAPI, React). With single-pass retrieval, 97% of cited URLs resolved, against 36% with no retrieval.
